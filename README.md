## Funnel Analysis using Power BI 📊
 I built an interactive Power BI funnel analysis dashboard to understand how users move through a website and where potential customers drop off before completing a purchase.
## Objective
 The goal of this project was to analyze the entire customer journey—from entering the website to completing (or leaving without) a purchase—identify key drop-off points, and provide data-driven recommendations to improve conversion rates.
## What the dashboard covers
 ✔️ Conversion rate and drop-off rate at each stage of the funnel using an interactive funnel chart
 ✔️ Identification of the stage with the highest user drop-off and lowest conversion
 ✔️ Analysis of multiple factors to understand why conversions drop at a particular stage
 ✔️ Monthly purchase trends to understand seasonality and performance over time
 ✔️ Country-wise comparison of total purchases
## Key business questions answered
 • What are the conversion and drop-off rates at each stage of the funnel?
 • Where exactly are users dropping off in the customer journey?
 • Is any specific factor influencing the low conversion rate on the product page?
 • How can the conversion rate be optimized so more visitors take the desired action?
## Key Insights: 
Out of 1,872 visitors who entered the website, 792 customers successfully completed a purchase. While most visitors reached the product page, a large number dropped off before adding items to the cart, making the product page the weakest point in the customer journey. This indicates that customers are interested enough to explore products, but something on the product page is preventing them from taking the next step. 
Multiple factors were examined to understand the reason behind drop-off: Device usage analysis showed similar behavior across mobile, tablet, and desktop users, indicating that technical or device-related issues are unlikely to be the cause. Traffic source analysis (Google, Email, Direct, Social Media) showed no major differences in visitor behavior, suggesting that traffic quality is consistent. 
## Business Recommendations 
To improve conversion rates and reduce drop-offs at the product page, the following actions are recommended:
* Make product information clearer and more benefit-driven to help users quickly understand the value.
* Strengthen trust signals by highlighting customer reviews, ratings, return policies, and guarantees.
* Provide transparent pricing details, including actual price and any available discounts or offers.
* Offer similar or related product suggestions to match user preferences and keep them engaged.
* Focus on product-based marketing campaigns through email and social media to reinforce interest and drive intent-driven traffic.
